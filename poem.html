For Danielle

Turned my world to May,

In just a few short weeks, you lit my days,

Unfolding joy in every path you tread;

Your radiant energy ignited all my ways,

A golden grace that everywhere you spread.

Now distant shores must carry you away,

And heavy sorrow rests on both our hearts;

Yet knowing you has turned my world to May—

A tender gift that never quite departs.

Take comfort, friend, for all will be alright;

No ocean dims the beauty of your soul.

My love and deepest care will cross the night,

To keep you safe and make the distance whole.

Across the miles, wherever you may roam,

I’ll hold you in my heart through all of life;

For in a brief, sweet spark, you felt like home,

A lasting light above the quiet strife.
